# Studio UI — the sketch preview page layout

Read before touching the sketch page's panels, the docked/floating layouts, the mobile drawer, the filmstrip or the export surface.

## The layout model (2026-08-28)

One inspector, a content rail, a filmstrip, export in the top bar. Decided with the maintainer over an interactive mockup (conversation "sketch-preview-interface"); the data model was explicitly out of scope — only placement changed.

- **Inspector (left)** — `SketchSettings`: a "canvas & animation" section (`RootSettings`, editing `format`/`animation`) above the sketch's own form, one action bar pinned at the bottom (reset / randomize / apply-to-all-slides · dev: save-defaults / thumbnail / preview · ui-sound far right). The panel exists even when the sketch has no `formConfiguration` — canvas settings must never disappear — so `SketchPage.reserveLeft` is simply `dockedDesktop`.
- **Content rail (right)** — `OptionsPanel`: "content" (the selected slide's — `SlideEditor`: transition + content items) above "shared content" (root `content`, which is simply "content" when there are no slides). Nothing else: general settings and the slides list both moved out. Like the inspector, the docked rail carries **no padding of its own** — its sections are full-bleed bands and must reach the rail's edges; anything that is not a section (the recording / import banners) pads itself. A `p-2` on the rail is the bug to look for when a band stops touching the edge.
- **Filmstrip** — `SlideFilmstrip`: horizontal thumbnails, dnd reorder, per-slide aspect ratio from `size` overrides. Docked: a band between the rails whose height travels through `--studio-filmstrip-height` (7rem with slides, 3rem empty, 0 otherwise) — SketchPage subtracts it from the viewport, the band sizes itself with it, the transport bar offsets above it. Floating: a card at the foot of the right column, under the content panel and the same width — with the deck there rather than over the canvas, nothing sits under the floating transport bar. The strip is `items-start`: a slide is a thumbnail plus a name line, so centring the row pushed the add button (which has none) half a line out of alignment. **The add slot is a slide, not a button** — a dashed tile the height and aspect of the slide pressing it would create (the active slide's `size`, the canvas's when the deck is empty, since a new slide inherits the active one). An empty deck is that same row with only the slot and a caption, and it carries the row's own padding: as a floating card sized by a bare pill it measured 32px tall, and a 16px radius on 32px reads as a pill beside the tall panels (66px with the padding and the slot). The docked band is 3rem when empty, so that host passes a smaller thumbnail height rather than letting the slot overflow it.
- **Transport bar (all layouts)** — `TransportBar`: play/pause · the scrubber (`AnimationProgressionBar variant="inline"`) · a snapshot camera · a red record dot. **It is a full-width bar along the bottom edge, not an island**: square corners, one top rule, no shadow, 3rem tall, identical in docked, floating and mobile. It became one when the floating layout had three islands competing for the bottom of the screen. Everything else is positioned off it — both docked rails and the filmstrip band stop on it, the floating islands and the mobile stack clear it by their usual margin, and `SketchPage` subtracts it from the viewport — so its height is stated once, in `--studio-transport-height` (`STUDIO_TRANSPORT_HEIGHT_VAR`, published by `SketchOptions`). **This reverses an earlier call** not to add a second height variable: that held while the bar rode inside the mobile stack, and stopped holding the moment five boxes had to line up against it. Being screen-wide, the bar also brought back readouts the pill had dropped — `frame / total · elapsed / duration · percent`, the last two hidden below `md` where only the clock fits. Snapshot and record sit together on purpose — one still, one moving image, the two ways out of the sketch; both go through `@/lib/canvasSnapshot`, which reads the engine's capture source rather than `getCanvas()` (DOM engines only refresh their mirror canvas on redraw, so a live read during playback returns a stale or blank frame) and is shared with `EngineControls`. It replaced the scrubber that used to be welded under the canvas and the capture card that floated bottom-right; **there is now exactly one scrubber in the DOM, in the transport bar**, on phone as on desktop. Because the scrubber left the viewport, SketchPage passes its `onSeekStart`/`onSeekEnd` down to `SketchOptions` — without them the viewport's performance label stops reporting "seeking".
- **Engine controls are identity, not playback** — after the transport became the bar, `EngineControls` (the top-left island, and the same buttons in the docked top bar) kept only the GitHub source link and the share/embed dialog. Play/pause and the snapshot both moved into `TransportBar`, where the scrubber they belong with already was; the dev **double-click that writes the sketch's catalogue thumbnail** (1x + 2x through `/api/dev/thumbnails/save-from-canvas`) moved with the camera into `SnapshotButton` — that is where to look when a thumbnail needs regenerating. Removing a control from that row means checking the new last child for an orphaned `border-r`: it has now bitten twice, on the camera and on the share trigger.
- **Capture is one dialog, several triggers** — `CaptureDialog` holds `CaptureActions` + options import/export; the record dot opens it, and in the docked layout so does the top bar's Export button (the only solid, ink-filled button on screen). **Its content stays mounted while closed** (visibility toggle, plus `pointer-events-none` on the closed container so it never eats clicks over the sketch): `captureActionsRef` is the autosave handle (`useFormState` calls `saveAsDraft` through it) and a running recording must survive the dialog being dismissed. Undo/redo sits beside Export; both portal from `SketchOptions` into a `DockedTopBar` slot (`actionsSlotRef`) because the bar belongs to SketchPage while these need the form context — same pattern as the zoom-controls slot.
- **The bottom stack is derived, not hard-coded** — the transport bar is the floor, then the filmstrip band, then the floating islands and the Interactive mixer, each offset computed from the one below (`transportHeight` → `railBottom` / `islandBottom` → `mixerBottom` in `SketchOptions`). Adding a layer means inserting one term, never re-tuning three magic numbers.
- **Floating mode is kept**, reorganised not removed: same panels as islands, capture card stays in the bottom-right stack (no top bar to host Export there). The maintainer plans draggable floating panels later — panels must not hard-code their own anchoring; the wrapper/container owns position.
- **Mobile** — **two floating cards riding on the transport bar**, the desktop floating layout stacked instead of spread over two columns: a wrapper (`absolute left-2 right-2`, bottom offset by the transport variable, `flex flex-col gap-2`) holds the deck card and the collapsible drawer. The deck is a card of its own and not the first row of the drawer's body — it is navigation, so changing slide must not mean opening the settings drawer, nor scrolling the panel that holds them; it reaches the drawer as a `deck` node. Two tabs only, Sketch (canvas & animation + sketch form) and Content. **Recording and export are not a tab**: the transport's record dot opens `CaptureDialog` as a bottom sheet — the same single surface the desktop uses, so nothing about capture is duplicated per layout, and the mobile drawer no longer statically imports `CaptureActions`/`OptionsImportExport` (it also stopped re-rendering on every keystroke, since `capture.options` was `methods.watch()`). The engine-controls red-dot shortcut and its `OPEN_EXPORT_DRAWER_EVENT` are gone with it. Its tab bodies carry **no horizontal padding**, same rule as the docked rails — the sections are full-bleed bands, and the banners pad themselves. The Sketch tab opens with "canvas & animation" **collapsed** on mobile only (`useCollapsibleStates( isDesktop ? undefined : { rootSettings: false } )`): the drawer scrolls inside `max-h-[50svh]`, where two unfolded sections pushed the option count several screens down. **The wrapper — deck plus drawer, the transport excluded since it has its own variable — is what `--studio-drawer-height` measures, in both states**: it is observed collapsed too, where the old drawer published `0` and let the canvas run under the pill. The wrapper must hug its content (no `h-full`, no `inset`) or it becomes an invisible pointer trap over the sketch.

## The selected slide is named once, by the filmstrip

2026-08-29 — The highlighted thumbnail is the only place the selection is named. Panels never repeat it: the inspector header is just "Controls", the content rail's section is "content" (vs "shared content"), and `SlideEditor` renders no caption of its own. Before this, one screen carried the name four times — `Controls · slide 2`, a `SLIDE 2 CONTENT` section, a raw `root.slides[1].content` line inside it (0-based, 30px under a 1-based header), and the thumbnail — which on mobile made two *tabs* claim the same object while the Sketch tab, editing `slides.N.*`, carried no marker at all. **The one exception is deliberate**: `SketchBreadcrumb` keeps ` · slide N` over the canvas, because it names what is *rendered*, not what is edited. **How to apply**: if a panel needs to say which slide it edits, the answer is that the filmstrip must be visible, not that the panel should print the name.

## "Document" is not a scope in the UI

2026-08-28 — With at least one slide, the selected slide is always what is edited; without slides, the root blocks are. This matches what the code already did (`effectiveActiveIndex` falls back to 0; `resolveEffectiveSketch` spreads `slide.sketch` over root `sketch`, so the root is never *read* once a slide covers it) and the maintainer's history: slides were added after the fact, root `sketch` is the defaults holder, and slides are expected to grow into a preset system (a slide ≈ a named variant). Consequences, all deliberate:
- **No Document/Slide scope switcher** and no "Doc" card in the filmstrip — both were mocked up and rejected as a third state nothing needs.
- **Rejected: blue override indicators / per-field link-to-document chains.** Mocked, then dropped: `ControlChrome`'s existing modified-state (label gains weight, per-field reset appears) is the only signal wanted. Do not reintroduce colour-coded override badges.
- **The 0 ↔ 1 slide boundary must be lossless** (`useSlideManagement`): deleting the last slide demotes everything (sketch, size, animation, content appended after root's, asset lists unioned, interactive merged slide-wins); adding a slide inherits the active slide's current settings, never `sketchFormValues` factory defaults; everything handed to `makeDefaultSlide` is deepCloned because `SlideSchema`'s `sketch: z.any()` passes references through (the first slide used to alias the root sketch object).
- Open: root `content` ("shared content") is editable in place in the rail; the maintainer may later want propagation via apply-to-all instead.

## Filmstrip ≠ timeline

2026-08-28 — Slides are a collection of variants, not a temporal sequence; a slide is on its way to being a preset. The filmstrip therefore stays a separate band from the `AnimationProgressionBar` scrubber. Rejected: merging them into one bar where each slide is a timeline segment — it would impose sequence semantics the model doesn't have.

## Panel sections: bands, not boxes

2026-08-28 — Every settings group is a `PanelSection`: a full-bleed header (uppercase, letter-spaced eyebrow + optional meta/actions + chevron) closed by a hairline running edge to edge. Sections read as a stack of bands, which keeps a long inspector scannable without boxing each group in its own card. The rule does the structural work — do not re-add per-section borders, rounded cards or background tints.

Depth is expressed by *treatment*, not by nesting boxes (`FieldRenderer`'s `nested-object` takes a `depth` prop, threaded from `GenericObjectForm`): depth 0 — a group directly under a section — renders as a sub-band with its own top hairline and a full-bleed, hover-highlighted header; deeper groups get a left indent guide and no rule. The previous style put every group in a `border rounded-xl` box with `ml-2`, which stacked borders inside borders as soon as a sketch nested two levels.

Two traps, both hit on the first attempt and both structural:

- **Padding lives on the leaves, never clawed back with negative margins.** A band cannot reach the panel edges with `-mx-3` — measured, it landed 25px short of a 320px rail, because the margin is relative to `FieldRenderer`'s own wrapper, not the panel's content box (the scrollbar and the section padding both intervene). So `PanelSection` takes a `bodyPaddingClassName` (`px-0` for form bodies) and `GenericObjectForm` applies `leafPaddingClassName` to leaf fields only; groups render unpadded and pad their own header and children. Bands then span edge to edge by construction.
- **A section never pads its own body when the content ends in a band.** `PanelSection`'s default `pb-3` shows up as dead space between a trailing collapsed band and the section's hairline, so form bodies pass their own `bodyPaddingClassName` (both axes) and `GenericObjectForm` adds the trailing space only when the last field is a leaf. Same principle as the horizontal padding: full-bleed content owns its edges.
- **Spacing between bands comes from the band, never from a container `gap`.** A `gap` on the fields container also pushes the bands apart, leaving each separator floating in dead space instead of reading as the top edge of the group it introduces. The container has no gap: leaf wrappers carry their own `py`, bands sit flush against their hairline (measured: 0px between consecutive band roots). For the same reason a band header carries no hover tint — the rule and the chevron already say it is interactive, and a tinted row reads as a selected item.
- **A rule marks every boundary a band takes part in** — above a band, and above the plain field that follows one; two plain fields in a row are a list and get none. `GenericObjectForm` decides this from the field's neighbours and draws it on an outer, unpadded wrapper, so the rule spans the panel even when the field inside is padded.
- **`first:`/`last:` variants cannot pick out a group.** Every `FieldRenderer` return is wrapped in its own `<div>`, so each group is always `:first-child` — a `first:border-t-0` silently removed *every* rule (`borderTopWidth` measured `0px` on all of them). Position is computed in the parent form instead. Assume the same about any positional variant inside this form tree.

Order inside the inspector: **canvas & animation first, then the sketch's own parameters titled "N options"**. The count used to sit in the panel header, where it described only part of what the panel held; it now titles the block it actually counts, and the header names the surface instead ("Controls" + the active slide).

## Collapse animations: never animate width or radius

2026-08-28 — **The floating panels do not collapse as a whole any more** — only their sections do, so the section titles stay on screen and a fully collapsed panel is already a compact stack of headers (measured: 320px wide, shrinking from 532px to 385px with one section closed). That removed the second, competing toggle on the inspector and the content card, and with it the morph below.

The floating inspector and the Interactive mixer used to swap `w-80` ↔ `w-fit` and `rounded-2xl` ↔ `rounded-full` between states. Neither is animatable (auto width has no interpolation), so the panel snapped into a pill while its height was still interpolating through `CollapsibleItem`'s `grid-template-rows` transition — a visible morph. Both now keep a **constant width and radius**; only the body height animates. **How to apply**: a collapsible floating panel picks one width and one radius for both states. Verified by sampling the box through a collapse — 320px / 16px constant, height 532 → 34. Sampling *intermediate* heights headlessly is not possible here: SwiftShader renders a sketch page at ~2fps, so a 200ms transition completes between two animation frames and every poll lands on an endpoint.

## One radius for every floating surface

2026-08-30 — All of them are `rounded-2xl` — 16px, measured: the inspector, the content rail, the deck card, the transport bar, the capture dialog and the mobile drawer (which was `rounded-xl`). When a short panel *looks* rounder than a tall one the radius is not the cause: it is a panel with no padding of its own, whose height collapses onto its content until 16px covers most of it. **Fix the padding, never the radius** — a per-panel radius is what makes a stack of islands look assembled from parts.

## Visual language (from the validated mockup)

2026-08-28 — Monochrome stays the rule: the Export button is an ink-filled pill precisely because nothing else is filled; red is reserved for recording-in-progress (the mobile record shortcut already uses it). The sketch name appears once — the breadcrumb above the canvas — never in the top bar. The full zoom cluster (−, %, +, 100%, fit/fullscreen) survives in the docked bar; do not collapse it to a single fit control.
