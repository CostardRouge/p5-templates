# A campaign per sketch

Read when you touch `src/lib/planner/campaign*.ts`, `beatStage.ts`, `beatVariant.ts`, `src/lib/variations/`, the variation mark on a gallery piece, `CampaignPanel.tsx`, or the `campaign.*` / `variations.*` / `process.build` commands. The decision and his five answers are in `publishing.md` («A campaign per sketch»); the Plan it sits in is «The Plan» there.

## A campaign is pins computed on every read, not a second planner (2026-11-20)

**Decision.** `campaign.ts` wraps `planSlots` without changing it: `layCampaigns` puts every beat on the calendar, `planWithCampaigns` hands each PROPOSED beat to `planSlots` as a pin on its slot, then rewrites that line's key (`campaign:<id>:<beat>`), title (`Materials · Smoke · Variations`) and why, and hangs a `BeatTag` on the proposal (`beatOf( proposal )` — a field of a type local to `campaign.ts`, so `planner.ts`'s `Proposal` is untouched). **Why**: a pin already bypasses the gap rule (a campaign repeats a sketch within days on purpose), marks the sketch `used`, and wins over the pattern; and a session was changing `planner.ts` in parallel (`claude/plan-queue-and-descriptions`). With no campaign the function IS `planSlots`. **How to apply**: a new rule about where a beat may land goes in `layCampaigns` or `blockedSlots`, never in `planner.ts`; anything that draws or answers the plan calls `planWithCampaigns` (PlanView, `readPlan` in `planCommands.ts`), never `planSlots` alone, or campaigns vanish from that reader.

**The laying rules** (each has a spec): campaigns in the order they were made; each beat on the first free slot whose LOCAL day is on or after `day 0 + offset`, strictly after the beat before it, that no other beat holds, with at most `CAMPAIGN_WEEK_CAP` = 2 of one campaign in a Monday-to-Sunday week (posts already out count). Day 0 is where the FIRST beat really lands — its post if one is known, else its slot — so a release moved to Friday moves what follows. A beat may not take a slot he skipped, a slot he pinned by hand, nor a recap's rendezvous (`blockedSlots`: a month closes once; met in the first spec, where «How it's made» moved from Tue 1 Dec to Fri 4). Free slots are read `CAMPAIGN_WEEKS` = 14 ahead (the comeback is 56 days after a release that may be five weeks away); only the Plan's five weeks become lines, the rest shows in the campaign's own list of beats. Postiz is read 8 weeks ahead, so a date beyond that is the rhythm's, not a checked one. With his templates and three slots a week the cap of two rarely binds — the spec proves the mechanism with `weekCap: 1`.

**While a campaign is live its sketch is the campaign's**: `drop:` and `favourite:` of that sketch (and `series:` of its category for a finale) are passed to `planSlots` as passed-over, so no pillar proposes it beside its beats.

## Which post is which beat (2026-11-20)

A beat must stop being proposed once its post exists — otherwise scheduling the release frees… the next slot for a second «Release». Nothing in Postiz names a beat, so: (1) opening a beat from the Plan records `opened[ beat ] = { at, slot }` (`markBeatOpened`); a post of the beat's key on THAT slot's local day is its own; (2) failing that, a single-sketch beat takes the first unclaimed post of its sketch on or after its day (a post made outside the Plan, or moved in Publish). A series reel and the words card are posted from the host every reel shares (`p5/empty-sketch`), so for them only rule 1 applies — an unopened reel beat never claims another reel's post. A claimed post makes the beat `queued` (ahead) or `posted`. **Opened is not posted**: an opened beat stays proposed («opened in Publish, no post of it known yet») until a post is seen. **Known gap**: a reel beat he schedules on another day than its slot is not recognised and stays proposed — leave it out by hand.

## The store is its own key (2026-11-20)

`campaignStore.ts`: `localStorage` `sketchbook.campaigns.v1`, validated on read (`readCampaigns`: id and sketch key by pattern, a known template, `off` and `opened` restricted to that template's beats, a campaign whose day 0 is 180 days old dropped, 40 at most), the `storage` event for the other tab, a cached array for `useSyncExternalStore`. **Not a member of `PlanChoices`** although the brief said «with the Plan's choices»: the Plan's «Reset» forgets passes, skips and pins and must not drop a campaign, and `planStore.ts` / `planner.ts` were being edited by the parallel session. «Another» on a beat's line is `passBeat` (the beat joins `off`; «Put back» in the campaign's list), not `passProposal`; «Skip» is the Plan's own and the beat moves to the next slot.

## Variations: a calculation that proposes, a mark that waits (2026-11-20)

**His answer (Q4)**: an agent proposes, pieces kept in the gallery, he keeps the good ones — never settings drawn at random into a post. Built as three parts.

- **`src/lib/variations/propose.ts`** (pure): candidates read from the sketch's own `formConfiguration`, each ONE named move — `palette` (a `palette-picker`, or a select whose key says palette / colours / theme), `choice` (any other select, one alternative of each before a second of any), `amount` (a ranged slider to 20 % or 80 % of its range, on its own steps, only when that is at least 15 % of the range away; sliders whose key says count / size / scale… first, phase / offset / opacity… last), `seed`, `toggle`. `proposeVariations` takes the moves in turn so six candidates show five kinds of change. Deterministic: an id (`palette:render.palette=sunset`, `low:text.handles`) is recomputed, nothing is kept between `propose` and `keep`. **Left out on purpose**: text, colours, easings, vectors, backgrounds, conditional groups, device and sound controls, the `interaction` / `bindings` blocks, and quality knobs (`quality`, `renderScale`, `supersample`) — the same picture, slower. A field hidden by another field's value is still proposed: the sheet shows it changed nothing. The dice (`sketch.randomize`) never makes a candidate.
- **The mark** (`GalleryEntry.variation = { state: "candidate" | "accepted", by, label }`, `types.ts`; `store.mark`; carried by `keepPiece`, the archive and `summarize`): `variations.keep` writes a CANDIDATE; only the person makes it `accepted` — in the Plan (the Variations line lists the sketch's variations with Accept / ✕) or on the sketch's sheet (every kept piece wears the control; «+ Variation» on a piece he kept himself accepts it at once, it is his own choice). ✕ on an agent's candidate DELETES the piece, on his own piece only takes the mark off. **There is no `variations.accept` and there must not be one** (a spec asserts no command id matches approve / accept / schedule / send).
- **`variationsDocument`** (`kept.ts`): accepted pieces only, oldest first, 3 to 5 for a release (1 for a favourite's «Variation») → the oldest piece's document with one SLIDE per piece carrying that piece's settings (`pieceSettings`: root under first slide). `beatVariant.ts` `routeBeat` then makes the post a CAROUSEL: a `Variations carousel` export variant (still, every slide) in the sketch's variant list, taken by every account of the draft; opening any OTHER beat of the sketch takes that routing back, because a sketch has one post draft. Seen on the stub: three `POST /upload`, one post, `post_type: post`, 3 media.

**Looking before keeping**: `variations.sheet` tries each candidate through the form (`setValue`, like a control), waits for it to land, redraws at one loop position, snapshots, and puts the form back — one picture, tile 0 the sketch as it is. **Trap**: the form hands its values to the sketch on an animation frame (`useFormState`), so a snapshot right after `setValue` shows the OLD picture; `landed()` waits three frames, or 500 ms where no frame comes (a hidden tab — where the values do not land either: the sheet then shows the same tile N times, which is the symptom). The whole command is one undo transaction and, the form being restored, leaves no undo entry.

## How it's made is the breakdown, asked of the sketch page (2026-11-20)

**His words**: «on peut se servir du breakdown pour le how it's made». The beat adds ONE `breakdown` content item (`PROCESS_ITEM_SEED`: `build.selection: "all"` — a sketch at its stock defaults has no diff to narrate, so `changed` would show nothing —, `placement: "roaming"`, `size: 24` because the schema's 16 px is a studio caption, unreadable in a 1080-wide post). **Why an ask and not a document**: the gallery knows only the catalogue, and a document without the sketch's own `sketch` block would replace its parameters with nothing; so `askStage` (sessionStorage `sketchbook.campaign.stage`, two minutes, beside `askPlanned`) tells the sketch page, which adds the item through the form on mount (`SketchOptions.tsx`, `withBreakdown` — never a second one). `process.build` does the same NOW for an agent. p5 only (`beatProblem` unticks it elsewhere and says why). Seen in a contact sheet of voronoi-v1: five steps (SITES, QUALITY, RENDER, INTERACTION MIX, BACKGROUND COLOR), the block in a different corner each, the cells settling. Which groups deserve a step is not decided: `build.excludeKeys` leaves one out.

## The other beats (2026-11-20)

`stageBeat` (`beatStage.ts`) is the one reading for the gallery's «Open in Publish» and `plan.open`. Release / comeback: the sketch as it is, `trial` written to the draft when the beat is a Trial Reel. Series reel: the Plan's own reel of the category (`seriesPool`), its usual words. **Words**: a splash card on the empty host headed by the SERIES' name over the line `YOUR WORDS HERE` (`WORDS_PLACEHOLDER`), no caption — his words are never written for him; the line says «Waits for your words on the card» but opens. **No longer true since 2026-11-21** (`postKey.ts`, `publishing.md` «Pass 3»): anything opened on the empty host from the Plan — a series reel, a recap, a campaign's reel, its words card — keeps its DRAFT under its own key (`p5/empty-sketch@<proposal key>`, a beat's being `campaign:<id>:<beat>`), staged by `stageReel` / `setCurrentReel` in `GalleryPage.openPlanned` and `plan.open`; what they SENT is still the host's, so the host's «Posted …» line mixes them. **In the render queue** only a release or a comeback is approved in place; every staged beat opens in Publish (`publishing.md`, «Approved lines and campaigns»).

## The templates and where they are offered (2026-11-20)

`TEMPLATES`: `drop` 0 Release · +3 Variations · +7 How it's made · +56 Comeback; `comeback` 0 Comeback · +4 Variation · +10 How it's made; `finale` 0 Release · +2 Series reel · +7 Words · +56 Comeback (the reel again). `templatesFor`: a favourite starts on `comeback` (and may take `drop`), anything else on `drop`; `finale` is offered for the highest version of a category with three sketches that can play — never the default, since the newest sketch of ANY series is its last so far. «Create its campaign» shows on a single-sketch line with no live campaign (not a reel, not a beat): the beats with their dates, each one unticked at will (his Full size is every beat ticked; lab 9's Light is unticking), then «Create · N beats». The release takes the place of a pin on its slot.
