## What Postiz really exposes (read in its source, `gitroomhq/postiz-app` `main`, 2026-11-20 — never against his instance)

`GET /public/v1/analytics/post/:postId?date=<days>` and `GET /public/v1/analytics/:integration?date=<days>` (`public.integrations.controller.ts`), both answering `[{ label, data: [{ total, date }], percentageChange }]`. For a POST: one point per label, today's total, as a STRING; `[]` when the post is not out, its provider has no `postAnalytics`, the token could not be refreshed, or the network refused (it does not say which); `{ missing: true }` when Postiz published it but lost its id on the network. **Per network, a post's labels**: Instagram (both account kinds) Views, Reach, Saves, Likes, Comments, Shares · TikTok Views, Likes, Comments, Shares · YouTube Views, Likes, Comments, Favorites · Threads Views, Likes, Replies, Reposts, Quotes · X Impressions, Likes, Retweets, Replies, Quotes, Bookmarks (nothing when the instance sets `DISABLE_X_ANALYTICS`) · **Bluesky nothing at all**. **Traps**: `percentageChange` is a CONSTANT in the providers (5 for an account, 0 for a post) — read by nobody here, and a test holds that; Postiz caches an answer an hour, and every miss calls the network with the account's token (X's counts against the instance's X app quota) — hence the sparing schedule; the public API's rate limit covers only `POST /posts`, so nothing but restraint limits these reads; an older Postiz has no such route and answers 404, remembered for a day (`StatsState.unsupported`). An account's series mixes daily points and single totals, label by label, per network.

## The rules every figure is read by

- **Attribution is the gallery's, unchanged**: a send of this browser knows its sketch and its piece (`Publication.pieceId`), a post from another device is known by the `Sketchbook` tag and the sketch its caption links (`foreignSends`). A post made outside Sketchbook is in neither list: never read, never attributed. Stories, drafts, queued and failed posts are never read.
- **Like with like**: a post's index is its settled headline over the MEDIAN settled headline of its own account («× your usual»); a send's is the median of its accounts'. A reach on Instagram and an impression on X are never added nor compared raw. **Not separated**: Reels and pictures of one account share one usual — splitting would double every floor.
- **Floors** (lab 8, Atelier's): an account has no usual under 5 settled posts (`MIN_USUAL`); a pillar, a series or a slot says nothing under 3 measured sends (`MIN_GROUP`) — its row shows its count and «too few to say». A post under 7 days shows its figures with «at N days · settles at 7» and is compared with nothing.
- **Never invented**: a figure a network does not give is absent, not zero (TikTok and Threads give no save, so no rate); the rate is worded with the network's own labels (`bookmarks + retweets per 100 impressions`).
- **A send's pillar is derived, not recorded**: the empty host → «Series & recaps» (the two cannot be told apart afterwards), else the sketch's star TODAY → favourite, else new. Recording it at send time would have meant touching the publish path.
- **Read sparingly**: once from a post's first day, once between 2 and 7 days, once from 7 — then never again by itself; 12 posts a run (`RUN_BUDGET`), a run when the gallery opens with the level on (once per page), a sheet's opening reads that sketch's unsettled posts at most once an hour each, «Read again» and «N more to read · read now» are gestures. Bluesky is never asked. Off, NOTHING is asked — verified by counting the stub's requests.

## The Plan that listens: one door, three bounded moves

**The door** is `PlanInput.signal?: PlanSignal` (`planner.ts`): `order( pillar, pool )`, `pillars( turns )`, `why( line )`. The planner VALIDATES — anything but a permutation of what it handed over is ignored — computes itself how far a sketch moved, and puts the signal's words at the end of the line's `why` (and alone in `Proposal.heard`, which draws the Plan's «Results» chip and reaches `plan.get`). The planner imports nothing of statistics. **The moves** (`signal.ts`): (1) a pool reordered by score — a favourite's own last measured send, else its SERIES' median once the series has 3 measured sends (the only way a never-posted sketch can move), else neutral — through `boundedOrder`: no sketch more than `MAX_SHIFT` = 3 places from where it stood, equal scores keeping their order (so a series keeps v1 before v2), and an index within `MIN_EDGE` = 1.2 of the usual counts as the usual; (2) when one slot leads the next by 1.2 AND one of the two pillars (new, favourite) leads the other, the week's turn of the stronger pillar is SWAPPED onto the stronger slot — one swap a week, a series' or a recap's slot never touched, the mix's counts exact; (3) the words. **Rejected**: letting a pillar's result change the MIX's proportions — the mix is his setting; a swap inside the week keeps it.

**The proof** (`src/lib/stats/__tests__/signal.test.ts`): on the REAL catalogue, the plan with the level off, with `show`, and with `listen` and nothing measured — or figures under every floor — is deep-equal to the plan with no signal; a signal that hands back garbage changes nothing; with data the order moves within the bound and returns the moment the level goes off. The moves themselves are tested on a small catalogue of their own, so adding a sketch does not break them.

**What it honestly does at the start**: almost nothing, by construction. 70 favourites and nearly all never posted lead the favourites pool, so a measured favourite cannot pass them by more than three places; the series score is what moves first (three posts of one series), then the slot swap (three posts on each of two slots, on an account with five).

## `stats.*` (seven commands, `statsCommands.ts`)

`stats.status` (always available; the level, what is kept, what each network gives, the rules), `stats.set` (the level — on the person's word only: turning it on makes the tab ask their Postiz), `stats.read` (the due readings, or one sketch's), `stats.sketch`, `stats.post`, `stats.summary` (`by` pillar · series · slot, `account` for raw medians; a thin row answers `tooFew: true` and no figure), `stats.accounts` (one request per account, on demand). All but the first two are `unavailable` while the level is off. `plan.get` gained `listening` (`{ level, measured, moving, note }`) and each line's `heard`. There is still no `plan.approve`.
